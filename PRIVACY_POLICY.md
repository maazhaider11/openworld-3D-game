# Auralis Frontier — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Auralis Frontier is an offline game. It does not create accounts or collect, share, or transmit personal information. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, or multiplayer services. The Online menu option is a Coming soon placeholder and does not connect to a service.

Village progress, army composition and preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage. You can erase this information using Settings → Reset village, or Android's app-storage controls. The game does not provide cloud backup or transfer of progress.

The game does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or personal media. It does not require internet access to play.

Any future online features will require an updated policy before those features are released.
